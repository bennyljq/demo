# V36 — Challenger: stroke termination and edge character

**Reject this treatment; retain V33 as champion and default.** Narrowing shoulders produced more pointed marks without a convincing reduction in distracting pigment boundaries on Rainier or Pillars. There is no meaningful demonstrated benefit at approximately equal compute. The app, champion implementation, Stable V12/V17 and existing V31 availability are unchanged. No failed planner was added to the selector. Human review remains the promotion gate.

## Cause and bounded treatment

Six fixed V33 contacts were examined through canonical replay, comparing the preceding canvas, deposited original and pressure-modified contact in pigment and common Neutral. Selection and exact contact parameters are in [diagnosis.json](../artifacts/v36/diagnosis.json): Rainier sky108/lake43, Pillars background147, portrait cheek/neck20, late portrait sharp contrast2944 and rooster feather4458 (zero-based operation indexes). The widest eligible contact inside each fixed source box was selected before challenger outputs; sharp contrasts additionally require a late operation and width below8. These contrasts establish useful narrow marks, rather than claiming a semantic lip/feather classifier.

Contact shoulders contribute to the visible pigment outline. Changing only their pressure profile changes that outline in local replay. Different colours between overlapping contacts and their curved directions remain visible; internal longitudinal texture remains inside the contact. Neutral independently strengthens height ridges, including foundation marks: a relief ridge is not sufficient evidence of a pigment defect. This small intervention supports termination as one contributor, not the sole cause or an exhaustive attribution of every final mark.

The challenger derives **one** alternative from the existing proposal winner, using existing `pressureCurve: [.25,1,.25]`. It keeps peak pressure, path, taper, opacity, colour/loading, texture seed, material and all other parameters. The eligibility rule excludes foundation, compound contacts, existing pressure curves and sustained contacts. There is no semantic gating. The original winner remains a competitor. The actual narrowed footprint is fully evaluated by the canonical objective; it replaces the winner only for a strictly greater gain, using the existing tie tolerance. No RNG draw, proposal increase, forced quota, objective change or second fit was introduced. Canonical material deposition and pressure metadata already support replay; no renderer/version change was needed. This is geometric shoulder narrowing, not a new longitudinal alpha-fade profile.

The hard extra-search bound is one full evaluation per proposal opportunity. Alternatives were evaluated/selected at 8810/1187 on Rainier,9077/1430 on Pillars,9327/2862 on portrait and8794/1061 on rooster. Selection includes proposals later rejected for insufficient gain; actual accepted modified contacts were1132,1293,2167 and946 respectively. Participation was substantial without changing the scorer. Direct extra evaluation time was approximately2.27–2.93 seconds; total work also changes because subsequent paintings and proposals diverge.

Visual assessment is Codex's provisional, nonblind inspection, not a fabricated human vote. Quality/readability, artfulness, stroke economy and compute were assessed separately, without a master score. Because the problem-case treatment visibly fails to deliver its intended benefit, rendering stopped after seed42. Seed73 confirmation was deliberately not run; there was no tuning or substitute experiment. Cross-seed artistic claims are therefore untested. The correct recommendation is **reject**, not promotion or an unbounded revision. Recover the isolated treatment from its archive if future review requires reproduction.
