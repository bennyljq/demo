Direct-reference access did not explain away V59's weak finish: B/D lose to staged construction on Tay/Mat even with broad tools decoupled from stage progression. This is evidence against these particular direct variants, not all direct-reference methods. The bounded construction pair improves staged values/edges and numerical qualification, but its components were not independently ablated. Portrait skin/hair still look patchworked; Rainier's quiet sky/reflections remain scratchy. V60 has not matched V58's finish.

The material can construct quiet ramp/form values through locally feathered loaded overlap without redistribution. Automatic placement/selection fails to use that capability reliably, and the narrow-gap study shows why soft contacts cannot replace hard boundaries. Pressure/contact continuity and scalar fitted quantity are distinct. Ordinary pointwise selection can prefer weak hatch loading while leaving the mean value wrong; do not infer that more texture, forced hatching or uniformly lower pressure will fix it.

C's approximately 25% Tay compute increase (3% Mat) buys a modest improvement over V59. Earlier qualification is not an artistic success or proof of better compute efficiency. Keep current gates/objective intact; do not silently revive filtered scoring, multiscale search, material overlays, semantic rules, tool catalogues or broad planner rewrites.

A carefully limited future return of blending remains justified as a hypothesis for demonstrated discontinuities in quiet passages, with reliable reference-supported light/edges protected. V60 itself has zero blending. First consider evidence-led overlap placement/loading and value-mass support; another experiment must justify its cost and preserve these controls.
