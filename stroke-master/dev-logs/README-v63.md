**V63 Routine steady loading experimental is selectable for human review.** V62 inadequately exposed controlled deposition; broader availability produces useful construction and improved Mat modelling, but does not establish an overall artistic or compute winner. V44 remains default, V50 Baseline and exact V58 Benchmark remain preserved. No promotion or commit.

Visually, broad tone forms sooner, but early 100/300 construction has conspicuous contact-shaped facets instead of convincing quiet passages. Mat42/73 gain coherent cheek/nose modelling and clearer likeness at 2800; forehead planes remain patchy and V50 remains stronger. Tay5600 is well modelled and somewhat cleaner, but softer, with no established gain in charcoal character. Fine eyes/hair/lips survive. Rooster gains denser body modelling; horizontal tail scars and pale edge contacts remain, so lower error does not establish cleaner feather detail or silhouette. **Rainier is a loss in passage character**: lower grain and error coexist with clearer hard facets in the sky/reflection and awkward snow planes. Mountain and tree structure remain recognisable, without achieving V50's quieter organisation. Displacing broken contact can improve coverage while exposing loaded boundaries; neither blanket softness nor higher accepted usage is success.
