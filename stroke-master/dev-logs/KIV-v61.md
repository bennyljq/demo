# KIV after V61

V44 remains default; V50 Baseline and exact V58 Benchmark remain preserved. V58 remains a strong human-review contender, especially Tay5600. V61 Long gestures experimental is not adopted. No automatic promotion or commit.

The V59/V60 no-blend experiment is concluded. Those treatments demonstrated quiet-value material capabilities in authored overlap studies, but automatic construction still lacked coherent loading/placement and produced facets, patchwork and weak light boundaries. Direct-reference access did not resolve that limitation. This does not establish that blending is inherently harmful or essential in every passage. V61 explicitly restores exact V58, including its blending, as a new causal base; it does not reintroduce blending into a no-blend identity.

V61 establishes that the native material can render sustained thin, curved and broad paths. The short guided tracing budget explained part of the observed range, while residual/profile termination and ordinary full-support selection explain the later short marks. Merely raising the cap is insufficient. The new tracer selects early broad gestures approaching half an edge, but sustained thin/curved defining construction is not established automatically. Most useful long thin/curved selected examples remain preserved V58 opportunities. Early fragmentation and subsequent correction remain visible; similar blend totals cannot prove avoided repairs.

Keep the byte-identical compact support route as experimental infrastructure for narrow long contacts and replay. Its local scoring/allocation savings do not establish whole-painting efficiency; complete isolated timings are near a tie with substantial Tay variation. Preserve the full material-support acceptance and cached-error agreement. Do not reward length, force outlines, add a second search, relax gates, revive multiscale objectives or infer quality from texture energy.

Retain V60 contact feathering/quiet-loading overlap in KIV. Its authored ramp/form capability does not demonstrate reliable automatic use, and its narrow-gap failure demands reliable light/edge support. No direct evidence made feathering necessary for V61; none was imported. Any future use must remain versioned and separately justified.

A future limited blending treatment remains a hypothesis for demonstrated discontinuities in quiet passages, with reference-supported light and important edges protected. V61 already uses the original V58 blending unchanged. First examine how coherent direction, contact loading and placement establish useful passages before assuming longer travel or less smudging improves a painting. Keep human review of likeness, form, artistic character and complete cost as the adoption decision.
