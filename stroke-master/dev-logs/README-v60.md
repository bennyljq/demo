# V60 - construction and direct-reference ablation

V60 is selectable as **V60 Staged construction experimental**. Direct-reference construction lost to the staged treatment on Tay/Mat, so C is selected; B/D remain hidden, reproducible ablations. This is a modest construction improvement over V59, with a substantial Tay compute cost and unresolved faceted facial planes. It does **not** match V58's finish. V44 stays default; V50 Baseline, V54 Challenger, exact V58 Benchmark and V59 remain available. No promotion or commit.

## What changed, and what the studies establish

Authored 66-contact ramp/form studies demonstrate a material limit in V59: scalar dose fitting cannot remove the contact's abrupt lateral and terminal boundaries. Native12 adds one optional loading feather **inside the existing contact support**: transverse loading falls through 65% of the half-width, entry/release through 16.25% of path travel. Only steadier-contact alternatives use it, with entry/release .015 and unchanged physical pressure [.95,1,.95], breakup .12 and load bias 0. Contact alpha remains a swept maximum with anchored paper tooth. It adds/subtracts material locally; it never exchanges, diffuses or filters canvas pixels. Feather zero reproduces V53 native10 exactly at native/2x. Erasers retain their original hard subtractive contact.

Authored ramp vertical-gradient mismatch falls **9.20 to 4.02** and rounded form **7.15 to 3.00**; native material images show overlap seams disappearing. These are diagnostic improvements, not complete painting scores. The three-contact gap study fails with the feather: dark-mass pixel RMS **25.38 to 54.29** because limited loading and softer boundaries leave the mass too light. Hard contacts and erasure remain available and necessary. In actual 300-contact production, ramp pixel RMS improves **28.43 to 26.74**, form **16.95 to 15.17**, but the gap is unchanged/slightly worse **37.89 to 37.96**. Production is still patchy: capability is stronger than its automatic use.

The second complementary behaviour reuses the native V53 pencil line and current-reference passage walk; no new path catalogue. The balanced angular slot (fast pencil slot, thorough angular slots) becomes a line at the signed tonal anchor, pressure [.88,1,.88], breakup .2 and load bias zero. Direction and stopping use 2D image passages, not inferred 3D contours. Long defining strokes, broad shading, ordinary structural proposals, lifting and erasure remain available. There are no forced hatch quotas or extra contacts hidden inside an operation.

A concrete scoring limitation remains: 24 parallel lines over a uniform target 190 have mean **232.84** after pointwise fitting, versus **229.28** with authored dose .65. Fitting wins raw pixel RMS **50.25 versus 50.72**, while leaving a weaker mean tone. Neither hatch is an adequate quiet value field. Selection can prefer weaker hatching; no second objective was introduced. Pressure controls physical pickup/continuity before the independent scalar dose fit. Universal low pressure, source-pixel copying, noise rewards, extra search layers and smoothing postprocesses were rejected.

## Causal comparison and selection

A is exact frozen V59. B changes only reference execution plus the required geometry decoupling. C applies the construction pair with unchanged V59 targets/gates. D applies the identical pair against the original reference. Search remains **3/7/12 single-contact candidates**, one scalar fit per candidate; no additional scoring, local refits or placement search. The ordinary pointwise objective, cache/acceptance math and paper are preserved.

Direct B/D bind attention, guidance, fitting and acceptance to the original prepared reference from operation one. Their target arrays alias that original; no blur targets are generated, no stage promotion executes. Final **5.5% global / 10% regional**, three accepted-evidence checks, is diagnostic only; editing continues. Direct geometry uses the existing stage-zero broad-width ceiling throughout, while original narrow/erase/line families remain available. Audit: the sole V53 stage-dependent geometric array is [.135,.11,.08,.06,.065] times long-edge for broad widths; line/erase widths and lengths, candidate schedules, reference-led paths and V58 local guidance are independent of stage. Attention keeps its every-third-proposal detail sampling and 64-draw residual selection. Budget/Continue never ages tools into a fine-only phase. The internal final-target index is not an executed Stage 5. The hidden direct mode has explicit metadata/readout and rejects promotion edits; imported direct artwork is labelled Original-reference ablation.

The pair's benefit is stronger with staged construction. This comparison does not isolate the two components of the pair individually, or establish that all direct-reference methods must fail.

C was frozen after development and before rooster42, Rainier42 and Mat73; recovery freeze.json records exact runtime hashes. No held-out tuning. Numeric thresholds and the five targets stay unchanged: global **11/9/7.5/6/5.5%**, regional **17/15/13/11/10%**, 95th-percentile regional safeguard, three qualifying checks every 32 accepted operations. A stalled gate is never relaxed or bypassed by budget.

## Work and complete wall time

Three sequential counterbalanced trials per Tay/Mat compare selected C, V59, V58 and V50. Wall includes prepared-input decode, initial analysis/target filtering, guidance, fitting, ranking, native raster, caches, accepted application, gates/transitions and Continue. Evidence metrics/I/O/replay are excluded. No other painting/browser workload ran during timing. Engine module loading is outside the timed session. Single development B/D timings are exploratory; they are not used as stable performance rankings.
