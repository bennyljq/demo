## One field and one renderer

Flat and Filbert only. Existing `colour` is A; optional `loading` contains version 1, space srgb, colourB, balance, softness and variation. Document v4 and brush/material versions remain unchanged because contact geometry and material deposition are unchanged. plannerVersion still means generation provenance: V14/V17 and historical records keep their labels; hand-authored lab studies leave it absent. New loading fields are bounded, finite and validated; unsupported profiles, unknown loading fields/version/space, loading on a compound parent and loading in older document schemas are rejected. Compound gestures keep loading on individual contacts. Older readers have no capability negotiation and can ignore unknown fields; replay these studies with V19 or newer.

The field uses the existing footprint-normalised signed lateral coordinate and canonical arc-length along coordinate. Balance maps [0,1] to a transition centre [-1,1]. Softness [0,4] both broadens a smoothstep transition and mixes towards the uniform ratio B = 1 - balance; 0 separates loads and 4 is an exactly uniform mixture. Correlated variation displaces the transition centre by at most .2 normalised half-width units, using two low-frequency sinusoids from the existing seed-derived pigment phase. No extra RNG draw, per-pixel random noise, stripe layer, longitudinal mode or persistent field array is introduced. Equal colours bypass field evaluation and match the old stroke exactly.

Interpolation uses encoded sRGB channel values. It is neither linear-light interpolation nor a physical pigment model; muddy intermediate hues remain possible. The smoothstep primitive analytically box-filters the lateral transition over the existing render pixel footprint, adjusted for pressure/taper. Larger exports reveal the same field, with narrower filtering and the existing adaptive geometry/fibre detail. Alpha-only validation skips loading validation/evaluation and returns before deposited colour calculations. Canonical geometry, alpha, pressure, coverage, depletion, pigment texture, height, relief and target feathering are preserved. Structural surfaces use this same broad field while omitting the same fine pigment variation they already omit.

## Fixed-contact proof

Three definitions were declared in the offline runner before evaluating any model: a 180x120 controlled tonal gradient; a 64x64 supplied-portrait crop at [326,126,64,64] with one Filbert contact; and a 180x120 tonal-to-near-white boundary at y=79 with one deliberately overlapping Flat contact. The supplied portrait is the preserved V18 reference derived from the user's original image. No whole-painting search, ROI selection after seeing gains, geometry search, budget expansion or production fitting hook was used.

Each pair fixes geometry, seed 4242, brush, opacity .8, load 1.1, dry .1, pigment .3, depletion .25, taper .2, substrate and the seeded translucent Filbert undercoat. Undercoat colours derive from each reference mean with fixed offsets; this artificial sparse starting point is not a previous V17 painting. Source, before, single and two buffers are shown in the gallery. This is an honest representation proof, not reconstruction of a portrait by one contact.

Fits minimise full-canvas native **material pigment RGB squared error**, including existing pigment, canonical deposition alpha and fixed fine pigment texture. An alpha render and neutral-colour material render recover deposition coefficients and pigment offset. Each bounded regression solves A/B channels with 24 projected coordinate updates per channel; colours stay [0,255]. Variation is zero. One single fit plus nine balance/softness starts and twelve local coordinate evaluations give **22 complete loss evaluations per case**. No exhaustive sweep or new optimiser dependency is used. Each evaluation performs a canonical basis render, actual material render/loss and a separate canonical structural render/fullScore diagnostic: 66 raster calls inside each fitting window, plus fixed preprocessing. Loss is checked on actual clipped Float32 material output, so analytical colour fitting never substitutes a uniform scorer for a rendered gradient.

All canvas pixels are evaluated, including the complete affected contact, pre-existing paint and applicable scoring halo (radius 1). Unchanged pixels add a constant error, and worsening neighbours are included, never masked out. Production fullScore is reported separately: it combines coarse/fine blurred colour, native colour, coverage and source importance. It is not the optimised SSE; local/full production scoring is tested on loaded feathered contacts.

## Visual assessment and limits

Ten gallery studies cover both profiles, straight/curved pulls, contrast/tonal pairs, a visibly split load and an exactly uniform mixed pair. Studies use seed 42 and bounded .08 variation; the lab independently provides reproducible seed changes and three previews. Pigment/Neutral and native/2x canonical renders share records. Equal-size checks show corresponding native and 2x images side by side, not different source references.

Tonal pulls maintain continuous cross-width transitions through the curve and release. A contrasting green/ochre pair has an intentionally muted intermediate hue; it is an encoded-channel colour mixture. The zero-softness study visibly separates loads; it can look like a band by design and is not a smoothing treatment. Smooth-gradient fitting reduces the abrupt uniform contact facet, while the contact edge and pre-existing fibres remain. Enlarged replay keeps the same broad loading pattern but exposes the existing fine ridges; this does not resolve V18's wider preview/material consistency issue.

The portrait contact adds a useful graded cheek/nose tone instead of a uniform blob, but leaves synthetic-looking contact shoulders and cannot reconstruct the nose, eye or lip structure from this sparse starting surface. Two-colour RGB worsens 380 pixels versus 418 for the single load; whole-region SSE includes those costs. Tonal continuity improves modestly; feature preservation is constrained by the fixed shape, and no anatomical fidelity claim is justified.

The boundary fit favours a white-loaded side and a sharp transition. It remains a contact crossing the selected boundary, including substantial alpha beyond y=79. Its two-colour result still worsens 1734 pixels versus the before buffer. Even where its colour moves towards the bright reference, support validity is false. Colour attraction cannot waive footprint discipline. Native and enlarged boundary images retain that spill. No validator threshold, narrowing or acceptance rule was relaxed.
