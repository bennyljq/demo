**Keep V22 as default. Retain Purist-v1 as an experimental synthetic block-in prototype; reject general promotion of this policy.** A readable still life does not establish generalization. ## Research access and decisions

The accessible text of Reynolds, Carder, Gurney and Gamblin was read. Embedded videos were not inspected. The supplied National Gallery page did not expose the requested technique discussion in its returned text. Its primary [Wilson/Wyld/Roy technical article](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/technical-bulletin/wilson_wyld_roy1981) was found; the large PDF could not be fetched through the browser tool, but its linked text-only RTF was downloaded and read. Technical examination describes superimposed layers often finished with opaque paint, partly wet-in-wet mixing, and direct use of white priming without a uniform overall coloured underlayer. Its discussion also describes broad abrupt marks and simplified forms. This supports discrete opaque construction as a plausible choice, while its wet mixing is absent from our renderer. It does not justify our thresholds, prove a universal method, or require a blank ground for all painters.

The [principles specification](V25-PAINTING-PRINCIPLES.md) separates documented practice, method disagreements, engineering interpretation, absent physics and hypotheses. Reynolds informs whole-frame construction; Carder informs extent checking and discrete colour placement but his object-completion order is not adopted. Gurney motivates considering boundaries independently of anchor similarity, while planned overlap/repair is deferred. Gamblin and the museum findings demonstrate that painting procedures differ; no glaze or wet-paint simulation is claimed.

## Minimal independent policy

`experimental/v25-planner.js` owns work selection, geometric extent search, block-in transition and native damage policy. It never calls V22's shortlist, coarse compatibility, coherent fitter, contour alternative, loading optimizer or schedule. Canonical straight gesture construction, reference scales/angles, affected-patch scoring with halo and deposition remain shared. Every visible mark is a recorded brush operation; no foundation image, stamping, clipping or smoothing exists. Materials, relief, loading semantics and defaults are unchanged.

Three broad scales cycle during block-in. Forty-eight regional probes compare sampled current/reference means. Bounded rays estimate colour extent and up to six Balanced candidates compete. Refinement adds two smaller scales but retains all broad scales. The transition uses relative structural error or four stagnation checks, not coverage, operation count or visual certification. A separate actual deposited native-pixel damage test rejects excessive neighbouring harm and protects reasonably accurate individual pixels, including narrow features. Canonical halo gain must also be positive. Targeted refinement stays on this policy. Search depth changes candidate count, not another planner. Fine target mode currently uses the same mixed-scale policy as Auto; focal/style control remains limited.

The sparse regional means and local safety thresholds are computational hypotheses. They are not descriptions of human cognition. V25 exposes its block-in/refinement phase in progress. Only target-checkpoint bookkeeping and that label changed after the main study: local work cannot change the global construction phase. Full global decisions repeat under the final module. Policy was fixed before decoding/inspecting the holdout; no subsequent production tuning occurred.

## Visual decision, separate from error

- **Synthetic:** both seeds establish the box, sphere, table and cast-shadow relationships. Early marks already place the masses; construction has readable composition and coherent light/shadow. Refinement reduces holes, but facets and hard contact ends persist. V12 produces a more continuous sphere; V25 has useful broad structure relative to V22, at roughly twice V22's measured work. Retain this bounded mechanism demonstration.
- **Beaver:** construction and refinement leave large pale mismatched interruptions across the water, subject and landscape. These are judged against reference relationships, not absence of canvas coverage. V12 is clearly more complete at equal operations. Purist's broad native protection and sparse regional selection reject many useful-looking proposals or fail to pursue missing masses effectively;34440 candidates include24817 damage rejections. That counter does not isolate whether selection, construction, averaging or guard thresholds are the principal cause. The block-in milestone fails on this practical test. Refinement does not repair it. Neutral shows additional straight-contact relief and seams.
- **Portrait:** face/eyes/lips remain recognizable; Purist connects cheek tones with smaller oblique facets, while V22 has broader directional planes. Proportions and forehead/neck modelling remain imperfect. Connected patches are a local merit, not established overall artistic superiority. V12 has lower error; Purist costs about86% more work than V22. Neutral retains faceting.
- **Rooster:** main silhouette and open toe gaps remain readable in the fixed crop. Crown/head accents and feather definition are simplified, and the background is unnecessarily patchy. The tested thin gaps do not show gross fill-in, but this is not boundary equivalence across images. Higher objective and roughly2.6× V22 work oppose adoption.
- **Holdout:** composition is recognizable, but face and shirt have conspicuous white/grey polygonal interruptions and flattened tonal transitions. This fails convincing tonal modelling despite correct broad placement. No tuning followed inspection.

The visual findings support retaining a small synthetic construction prototype and revising the independent policy, not adopting it or layering further repairs into this iteration. Neither V24 nor Purist-v1 establishes generalized broad recovery. Artistic character can coexist with missing structural relationships; the gallery retains both useful and failed examples.
