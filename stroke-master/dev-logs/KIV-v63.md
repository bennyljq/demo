# KIV after V63

V44 remains default, V50 Baseline and exact V58 Benchmark remain preserved. V58 remains a strong human-review contender. V63 Routine steady loading experimental warrants comparison, with mixed artistic and compute results; no promotion or commit.

V62's below-.5% accepted exposure was insufficient to conclude that controlled deposition cannot improve construction. V63 removes that restrictive eligibility gate from one existing deposit opportunity, with exact V58 geometry, other tools, original blending, targets/gates, attention and scoring. Steady loading is now accepted in 20.8-26.2% of operations, improving useful tonal coverage and Mat42/73 facial modelling. Several mixed-sign / varying-target shared candidates earn their fit; others remain useful but lose to a better existing operation. This is an availability finding and a bounded useful construction improvement, not a general victory over V58 or V50.

Firm controlled loading also exposes incidental contact facets. Early construction is more patchwork-like; Rainier's sky/reflection character loses despite lower diagnostic error and grain. Tay's cleaner finish is softer without a demonstrated charcoal-character gain. Rooster modelling improves while tail scars and edge contacts persist. Mat costs 9.7% more complete wall despite fewer sample visits; Tay saves 3.0%. Feathered development is faster on Mat but has no clear artistic advantage and was not selected retrospectively after held-out review. Preserve both hidden controls. No quotas, objective changes or further eligibility loosening.

**Spatial organisation is the next investigation**: align useful deposits across a passage and its boundaries so improved pickup does not merely form clearer isolated patches. Use concrete recorded states to separate incompatible geometry, local competition and lasting tonal gains. Do not infer repair cycles from blend totals, reward smoothness, force texture or launch another material/search rewrite. Loading availability itself is no longer the principal limitation.

The V59/V60 no-blend experiment remains concluded. Continuous pickup and feathering are verified capabilities, now tested alongside exact V58 blending. No-blend planners did not establish finished-quality parity, and direct-reference access did not solve that deficit. Zero blending is neither an artistic score nor evidence that blending is harmful. V60's separate pencil-slot change remains deferred.

Retain V61's verified long thin/curved/broad gestures and byte-identical compact-support engineering. Longer reach did not establish a painting-quality or economy advantage; V63 imports none of its planner changes.
