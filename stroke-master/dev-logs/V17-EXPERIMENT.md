# V17: identical V16 painting with cheaper validation

**Retain the optimization; keep V14 default pending user review.** V17 produces identical V16 strokes and painting buffers under matching settings. Median full-rooster step work falls 21.0%, from 31.720 s to 25.056 s. At the current quiet-body .25 default, the short-rooster median falls 14.7%, from 5.178 s to 4.417 s. Neither gain comes from changing material settings. The selector now contains only V14 and V17; new V17 records identify planner 17/material 2. The accepted V16 painting quality, including its remaining compatibility-admitted toe artifact, is unchanged.

**Measured bottleneck.** Temporary instrumentation of the historical .65, 2100-operation rooster attributes 1.252 s of 2.177 s inclusive validation raster time to profile-table calculation, about 57%. Geometry construction is only .004 s; support-surface creation is .035 s. There are 6477 initial checks and 259/196/168 subsequent attempts: only 623 of 7100 renders are retries. They are not the dominant cost. Validation allocates and initializes RGB/coverage it never uses, computes full pigment/height profiles and deposition values, and finishes rendering failed contacts before scanning compatibility. Candidate scoring then performs another canonical raster with padded blur halos and an existing structural surface. Sharing that surface would complicate lifetimes and representation; no scoring cache was introduced. Function timings are inclusive and overlap; instrumentation overhead is excluded from all speed claims.

**Small retained change.** The canonical rasteriser now accepts a support predicate. This uses the same segment projection, Float32 along/cross/endpoint maps, footprint, deposition alpha and target feathering as painting. It omits the unused RGB/coverage surface and colour/height writes. Shared profile/deposition functions accept an alpha-only mode, computing only alpha-required channels; profiles whose alpha does not read a table retain just the pixel-footprint value. Required Float32 profile filtering remains identical. An incompatible substantial-support pixel terminates validation immediately, after canonical geometry maps are complete. Passing contacts still inspect every relevant raster pixel. There is no second rasteriser, heuristic sampling, cache, persistent scratch buffer or altered tolerance.

The comparison reproduces the old zero-initialized coverage calculation exactly: `Math.fround(1-(1-alpha)) > .08`, rather than comparing an unrounded alpha. Narrowing remains .72, at most three times after the initial check, with floor .65; exhausted contacts still collapse to an empty cubic and receive canonical zero gain. Coherent fitting, shortlist/order, RNG, acceptance, palette, foundation, scheduling, analysis, material and lighting are unchanged. V17 accepts only single contacts, matching V16 construction; historical multi-contact documents use ordinary canonical replay.
