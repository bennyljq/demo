Tay stays in Stage 1 through 2800 and enters Stage 2 only at 3040; Mat enters Stage 2 at 1760. Existing coherent geometry and structural anchors help, but cannot by themselves quiet the material residual.

One steadier-contact alternative in freed blend slots repairs much of this deficit without redistribution. It produces recognisable portraits and preserves deliberate accents, but overlapping contact boundaries leave faceted skin, scratchy hair and uneven light planes. Extra texture and zero blends do not establish artistic improvement; V59 has not matched V58's finished modelling.

A future carefully limited return of blending is justified for demonstrated residual contact discontinuities and quiet passages, with reference-supported edges/light protected. This is a hypothesis from the three-way comparison, not proof that every blend is useful or a mandate to retry V57's rejected operator. V59 itself never reintroduces blending.
