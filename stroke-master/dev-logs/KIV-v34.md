# V34 KIV — frozen experimental baseline

Freeze V33 as preferred experimental development baseline; V31 remains the cheaper alternative. Stable promotion needs broader artistic confidence, not another numerical pass. V27–V29 regional construction remains paused.

Observed on both new references: pigment-level gradient/contact steps remain after facial fidelity improves; Neutral relief adds visible ridges, especially on skin and coat. Forest seed42 early eye readability is mixed, and fine foliage stays simplified. These are evidence-backed limitations, not authorisation to tune this validation set. If future work specifically needs smoother modelling, one bounded contact-band continuity experiment should compare fixed crops and pigment/Neutral while protecting the preferred portrait's nostrils and lips. Do not combine it with feature rules or brush expansion.

Keep automatic artistic stopping deferred: 4,000→8,400 still develops eyes, mouth, curls and boundaries, so error or operation count alone is not an artistic stopping verdict. Also defer stroke-end shaping/direction, relief/material changes, wet mixing, palette constraints and expanded brushes. Each would require a separately scoped question and evidence. No new algorithm is needed merely because the release number advances.
