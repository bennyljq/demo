# V25 research-to-code specification (before study review)

Purist-v1 is one observational, mass-first computational approach, neither a universal painter's method nor a trained model. Painting quality precedes cost. This specification and study selection precede output inspection.

## Documented practice and disagreements

- [Louise Reynolds, blocking in](https://www.jacksonsart.com/blog/2025/08/22/the-blocking-in-technique-for-painting-and-drawing/): broad placement, form, tone and colour make composition assessable before detail. Her plant demonstration builds background and plant colour masses, then smaller highlights; a tonal demonstration builds light, middle, then dark tones. Computational consequence: unresolved relationships across the whole frame take priority during construction. Ground showing through can be intentional; coverage is not success.
- [Mark Carder, first object](https://www.drawmixpaint.com/classes/online/step-8-painting-your-first-object.html): usually finishes one object at a time, dark to light, checking the spatial extent of colours and avoiding premature blending. This differs from our whole-image construction order. We take colour-extent checking and discrete marks, not his complete object ordering. No semantic object detector is implied.
- [James Gurney, painting across edges](https://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2018/02/painting-across-edges.html): opaque marks can cross preliminary outlines, with background/foreground painting in either order and later cutting back. Consequence: V22's anchor-colour compatibility is not treated as a universal artistic rule. Planned crossing/repair is deferred; current strokes must independently protect existing accurate features.
- [Gamblin, indirect painting](https://gamblincolors.com/indirect-painting/): transparent initial drawing, opaque middle and glaze can be distinct layers; dark-to-light and light-to-dark variants exist. We choose ordinary opaque loading. We do not model glaze chemistry, drying, solvents or fat-over-lean.
- [National Gallery painting page](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/claude-monet-bathers-at-la-grenouillere) and [Wilson, Wyld & Roy, Technical Bulletin 5, 1981](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/technical-bulletin/wilson_wyld_roy1981): the technical article is the relevant primary research, rather than an inferred account of a linked video. Access details and any technical findings actually read are recorded in the experiment report.

Only accessible text/demonstration descriptions were used; embedded videos were not watched. These sources do not establish our numeric thresholds or search strategy.

## Engineering interpretation and stopping rules

Share canonical gesture construction, deposition, material2, lighting, native analysis scales/angles, structural patch objective (including halo), session limits, target feathering, replay and export. These are rendering/geometric services. Do not call ContactConstruction, CoherentPlanner, V17 validation, V22 contour/loading shortlist, compatibility gates, location selection or stage scheduler.

Purist selects one scale per proposal, cycling radii derived from .12/.065/.032 of the image edge during construction; .016/.007 are added during refinement. The broad three remain eligible indefinitely. Forty-eight deterministic regional probes sample nine target/current points; rank squared difference between regional colour means. Existing blurred reference scales approximate major masses. Four axes with bounded sixteen-sample rays on both sides estimate colour extent; construct straight canonical strokes along up to two axes (one Fast, three Thorough), at full/half/quarter width. Ordinary averaged colour, no palette or loading optimiser, no anatomy/asset coordinates. Native reference and field direction help fine geometry. Analysis masks never determine eligibility.

Every 64 proposals, compare the shared structural objective with the preceding check. Transition from construction when below 18% of initial error OR four checks improve less than 1% each. This is a bounded stagnation/progress approximation, not visual certification of block-in. Existing operation/proposal/contact/memory safety limits and 256 consecutive rejected proposals bound search. Continue stays on Purist and preserves its phase; broad tools survive. Refinement uses the same search policy, with finer scales added. Targeted passes use the same policy with shared target feathering and regional probes. No hidden switch to another planner.

Each candidate is actually rastered into a structural patch using canonical deposition. Evaluate every changed native pixel, including contact ends and neighbouring pixels. Weight positive/negative native squared-error changes by 1+4*importance. Reject if total harm exceeds 30% of benefit, or if any already reasonably accurate pixel (normalized RGB SSE < .012) regresses by > .018. Require positive shared halo objective gain as well. This permits texture variation inside unresolved masses while protecting existing thin features. No promise of future repair, clipping or global-gain-only acceptance. The limits are hypotheses, not paint practice. Structural deposition matches canonical coverage; pigment/relief views are separately judged.

Renderer capabilities absent: wet pickup/physical pigment mixing, drying states, realistic glaze transport, planned multi-stroke repair and artistic focal hierarchy. Material and relief equations remain unchanged.

## Predeclared study

Before tuning: synthetic geometric still life (box, sphere, table and cast shadow), supplied beaver, supplied portrait, established rooster. Use existing Chrome-derived V24 source PNGs (beaver/portrait512x640, rooster632x461), identical per planner; synthetic256x192. Seeds42/73 on synthetic;42 elsewhere. Hold out existing `assets/mat.jpg`, selected by filename only, decode/inspect only after policy is fixed. Working edge640, Balanced/Balanced, body.25, ordinary enabled defaults, Neutral. Compare V12, V22, V25 at early300 / construction1400 / refinement2800 accepted operations (synthetic120/600/1200). Proposal cap four times each budget; report shortages rather than assume completion. No targets in quality comparisons. Show pigment/Neutral, whole images and fixed crops; neutral A/B/C labels with reveal. Measure step work and equal measured-work prefixes, analysis/export time, candidate/contact work, process RSS/array buffers and conservative engine memory estimate. Timings are local observations, not efficiency claims.

Acceptance: recognizable composition and coherent colour/value masses first; judge proportions, silhouettes/gaps, tonal coherence, useful detail and painterly character separately from numeric error. Inspect synthetic first to explain mechanism. Retain only a useful milestone; revise/reject refinement if it fails. No image-specific production tuning.
