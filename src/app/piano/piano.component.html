How scoring works

Perfect attack: 100 points. Good attack: 70. Miss: 0. A successful hold adds up to 100 more, proportional to held song time. A release within the final {{ settings().holdReleaseMs }} ms at the selected speed receives full credit; the allowance never exceeds half the hold. Wrong keys break combo without a point deduction.
