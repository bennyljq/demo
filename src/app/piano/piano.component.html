How to Play

Follow the letters

Read the stable passage above the roll. Each letter approaches the vertical playhead; press its key when it reaches that line. Spaces need no input.

The thin bar above a letter shows a hold: keep its key down until the bar fills. A correct attack earns {{ scoringPoints.perfect }} points within {{ settings().perfectMs }} ms (Perfect), or {{ scoringPoints.good }} within {{ settings().goodMs }} ms (Good). A missed attack earns {{ scoringPoints.miss }}. Each Wrong key costs {{ -scoringPoints.wrong }} points and breaks combo; score cannot display below zero, but debt carries forward. A completed hold adds up to {{ scoringPoints.hold }} sustain points; the final {{ settings().holdReleaseMs }} ms have a release allowance.

Adjust metronome and speed in Settings. Watch the actual Twinkle opening, then try it yourself.

For Twinkle, dashed notes show the intended melody. Solid green, orange and red notes show your Perfect, Good and altered-pitch Wrong performances.
