## Visual findings

- **Beaver:** body/log/landscape planes connect more clearly, with fewer pale interruptions initially and finally. The gain survives equal work. Later baseline strokes develop the masses rather than erasing them. Pale water/sky contacts and local gaps persist; this is not scene completion. Foundation-like colour is not proof of absent paint: foundation already paints the whole canvas.
- **Rooster:** tail/body blocking is more solid initially and at equal work; neck/feather detail develops later. Feet remain narrow and imperfectly modelled; crown/head accents change. Pigment/Neutral inspection shows no obvious large extra ground halo, filled leg gap or crown spill. That is visual evidence for this image, not a boundary guarantee. Relief/contact ridges persist.
- **tay-gradient:** cheek/neck tones connect with fewer horizontal fragments; equal-work crops retain the improvement. Forehead/nose facets and broad contact ends remain, and initial modelling is strongly faceted. Neutral retains directional slabs/ridges; no general relief claim. Double loading remains active:158 accepted V30 loaded contacts versus178 V22. Eligibility/fitting limits and zero variation remain; no donor is loaded.
