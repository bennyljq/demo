# KIV — V33

Use per-reference fidelity/time evidence and the existing V32 visual evidence when considering graded construction; lower structural error does not establish better art.

- Stroke ends/shoulders and directional bands.
- Relief and material refinements.
- Wet blending and physical pigment mixing.
- Limited palettes.
- Expanded brushes.
- Further compute work, only with profiling and exact-output verification. Do not add approximate pruning, altered fit counts, acceptance penalties, loading shutdown, GPU/WASM or worker migration to this iteration.

V27–V29 regional construction stays paused. V30 and V32 generation are archived through the frozen incoming Git control; their records/replay and shared canonical dependencies remain. Do not revive abandoned optimisation branches or duplicate painters in the normal selector.
