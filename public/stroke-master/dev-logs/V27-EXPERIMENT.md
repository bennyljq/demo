There is no demonstrated benefit-versus-damage improvement or retained broad treatment win. This is a negative construction result, not a rejection of every region-informed method. Canonical structural raster rebuilds RGB independently of material pigment/height; `fullScore()` rebuilds errors/tiles. Canonical `location()` samples isolated state. Fixed evaluation crops route anchors into body/water/boundary or interior/boundary buckets. The first two eligible distinct cell sets per bucket are retained within 256 draws/state, without treatment scores. Beaver300/1400 and rooster300/1400 yielded 6/2/3/2 regions;279 sampled anchors were flood-ineligible. Late beaver boundary and rooster interior quotas remained unfilled. Overlapping regions across prefixes are not independent trials.

Refinements are offered even when the live scheduler would not trigger them. No padding or canvas commits occur. The common halo scorer and gain>1e-6 acceptance remain unchanged.
