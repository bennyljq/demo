# V24: native interior width does not establish broad recovery

**Reject promotion of this treatment. Keep V22 as the development default and V24 as a reviewable experimental option.** The trace supports a specific false early width restriction, but removing that restriction for one candidate does not reliably complete coherent masses. No second treatment was added.

## Cause and distinction

The diagnostic observes the unchanged V22 trajectory, taking the first twelve attempts per blocking/shaping pass in each fixed beaver body/river and rooster boundary region. It records requested width, anchor-normal failures, initial path length and tracing stops, coherent fitting, canonical support and the final contacts. A same-path, same-colour nominal-width counterfactual isolates width restriction; it is diagnostic only, not an extra production candidate.

The anchor-normal ray restricts width using RGB distance to the anchor in an existing coarse reference (100 during blocking, 64 during shaping) and soft-mask difference <.45. The 1.12 margin then reduces width. Nearby boundaries influence the coarse RGB even when both native samples are in the same interior. At beaver shaping anchor (143.5,310.5), coarse anchor [151.88,100.41,65.43] versus sample [115.99,61.44,28.83] gives distance64.39 and cuts requested width about15 to1.7. Native values are [121,55,20] and [113,53,21], with mask difference.151. Same-path nominal construction survives unchanged fitting at width about3.3 with positive gain3.42. Rooster (342.5,309.5) has coarse distance82.14 but native [8,3,9] versus [13,4,7], mask difference.067; width about13.6 becomes1.5, while unchanged fitting permits about3.0. These are measurable false early exclusions, rather than absent anchors or solely a stage-allocation problem.

Other cuts are warranted. Beaver river (421.5,515.5) encounters mask difference.533 on its initial normal; the wider footprint later encounters native [114,133,122] versus anchor [218,210,182] and mask difference.836. White water, dark wood and gaps cannot be represented by that uniform contact. At body (142.5,347.5), nominal12.04 becomes4.03, while the counterfactual fits at12.04 with gain2.44 and native component standard deviation41.1: real internal colour variation, not a uniformly flat interior. Coherent fitting and final support may still narrow nominal alternatives, and tracing retains short paths at poor anchors. Native similarity along one normal does not establish that the full footprint is safe. The complete fixed trace preserves unsuccessful examples and precise triggering samples.

V12 is authentic planner11/material2 from the original executable. Its larger contacts also reflect a different, less restrictive construction/support search; its output is a behavioural reference, not proof that stage allocation alone explains the difference. Its matched seed42 early300/initial1400 beaver comparator is provided. V12 code was not spliced into V24.

## Single treatment

V24 preserves the three ordinary contacts, V22 contour sibling, RNG decisions and loading capability. Only in passes0/1, if the requested width is at most128 and the initial contact lost >20% of it, V24 offers at most one wider sibling of the original contact. Half-pixel native RGB/mask samples follow connected runs along the original anchor normal (at most128 reads per side). The original thresholds and 1.12 margin apply. Path, brush, uniform colour, material and texture seed remain identical. Shared coherent fitting and the original coarse-reference actual-support validator remain unchanged. An additional native canonical-footprint check rejects erased narrow features; it is bounded to a16384-pixel patch. Ordinary candidates remain eligible even if the sibling fails.

The new sibling receives no additional loading fits. Existing V22 loading retains one contact/two fits and its patch cap. Canonical geometry, deposition, objective, acceptance threshold, stages, attention and materials are unchanged. No clipping, reference underlay, semantic coordinates, global threshold increase or V23 periodic Fine probe is present. Continue stays on the original Fine schedule. This deliberately leaves final-fitting restrictions untouched; widening early construction cannot guarantee broad final deposition.
