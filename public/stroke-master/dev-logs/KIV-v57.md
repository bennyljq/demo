- **V56 scope:** one bounded transverse placement refinement using V53 material/proposals, simple pixelwise scoring and V54 early gates. No V54 Stage 5 tolerance or V55 scoring/fitting/promotion. V56 improves some contacts and modest final fidelity, but final character changes little and complete compute rises materially (about 58% on Tay and 32% on Mat in its recorded trials); retain its placement diagnosis and defer the extra search; it does not earn promotion. Immediate score improvement is only causal evidence, not a claim of improved complete art. Final quality, repair and complete compute decide the result; see [README-v56.md](README-v56.md).
- **V57 selective blending:** fixed distant exchanges can create disconnected lattice residues on compact marks. A masked continuous tent removes that defect and a reliable synthetic gap guide limits cross-edge transport, but complete paintings lose fidelity and character, with later qualification. Reject this one replacement; retain the diagnosis, not an automatic new blending treatment. Ordinary winning blends often recover meaningful structure. No quota, blanket ban or preservation of arbitrary canvas edges. See [README-v57.md](README-v57.md).
- **Next distinct deferred direction - structural versus tonal planning:** treat structural accents and tonal shading as distinct proposal tasks on one replayable canvas. This does not imply separate composited reference layers, stamps or a forced family mix.
- **Future stopping/texture preservation:** require concrete native-image evidence and quality safeguards. More grain, less blending, earlier promotion or a lower pixel error are not automatically better art.

Research references (applicability, not implementation mandates):

- [Decoupling strokes and high-level attributes for interactive traditional drawing](https://people.csail.mit.edu/fredo/PUBLI/Drawing/): separates stroke placement, tone, detail and smudging in an interactive system with semi-automatic tonal modelling. Useful design context; not an autonomous planner or evidence that our proposals will succeed.
- [Combining sketch and tone for pencil drawing production](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~leojia/projects/pencilsketch/pencil_drawing.htm): complementary sketch and tone construction. Useful structural/tonal distinction; image synthesis is not directly our sequence of replayable material operations.
- [Stroke-based rendering: from heuristics to deep learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00595): survey of heuristics, optimisation and learned approaches. Useful map of methods; no prescribed charcoal material, objective or acceptance criterion.
- [Primitive](https://github.com/fogleman/primitive): local shape mutation and fitting/scoring ideas. Use bounded comparisons as inspiration; geometric approximation does not solve charcoal character and does not justify restoring rectangular deposits.
