# V65 - progressive drawing through overlapping tonal passages

**V65 Overlapping passages experimental is implemented; the finished-drawing goal remains unmet.** Coverage and connected tone improve decisively over V64 on Mat and Wonder Lake. Ayam remains blunt; forest and Rainier expose severe late rejection/compute failures. V50/V58 remain much stronger finished drawings. Oil, frozen V64 and historical planners are preserved; V44 stays default. No promotion or commit.

## What changed

Quantised value regions no longer organise construction. Overlapping hexagonally distributed seed supports grow through spatial continuity and stop at reliable value boundaries, rather than equal-value bands. Each passage stores connected support, overlap neighbours, reliable/open boundary evidence, protected-light constraints, a plane or quadratic tonal model, intended direction/coverage and unresolved variation. Quadratic fitting is used only when it materially improves a poor plane fit. Complex residuals remain available for smaller modelling work. Frozen V64 curve extraction is reused; its quantised region output is discarded. No V50/V58 finishing fallback or generated target sequence exists.

Image-direction evidence guides contact groups where coherent. Elsewhere, support extent or the composition diagonal supplies a coverage convention; this is not an anatomical surface-direction inference. That convention still leaves conspicuous diagonal character in some drawings.

A passage owns a complete overlapping sweep, revisited with offset coverage. Real reference boundaries and protected lights may narrow a contact; artificial passage limits do not automatically pinch widths to zero. Contacts extend through open support ends where reference continuity permits. This corrected the rounded contact islands seen in the first sparse development runs. Paths remain long independently of width; they are not pasted regions or enlarged rectangular fills.

Up to eight native13 actions are fitted on a **local real-material patch**. Independent sequential fits initialise quantities; two bounded coordinate sweeps then adjust each quantity against the entire nonlinear sequence, accounting for the other contacts' overlap. Masks are reused within the task. The homogeneous deposit/lift case uses the exact product derivative; mixed sequences retain the general chain derivative. This is a coupled task fit, not a new candidate slot or a contact-loading/material experiment. Neither tonal models, support masks nor reference pixels are rasterised onto artwork.

Task acceptance records tone, reference-boundary derivative error, protected-light damage, quiet-passage discontinuity and per-action prefix evidence. Quietness is constrained separately from tone gain; there is no combined artistic score. Prefixes receive a light-damage constraint and are exposed at arbitrary operation checkpoints, but this first version does not establish that every prefix is visually convincing. Blending is conditional on existing coverage and measured seams, rather than mandatory preparation. Lifting and the native crisp eraser are available for light/edge recovery; their actual accepted use is reported, not assumed.

Coupled adjustment changed 192 of 694 accepted Mat tasks and 93 of 693 Wonder Lake tasks. These are local counterfactuals against independent fitting, not a full-trajectory ablation. No photographic run accepted crisp erasure, a selective-accent task or a directional-detail task. Their routes exist, but exposure was insufficient; most late transition work did not become effective finishing. [Recorded evidence](../artifacts/v65/causal-evidence.json).

## Progression and visible evidence

A distributed light scaffold is selected by composition-cell placement debt. Curves remain eligible for later correction, reinforcement or lifting. Passage priority combines broad-value deficit, neighbour contrast disagreement and recency, rather than only the largest local residual. Broad completion compares continuous models of reference and current material; unresolved fine variation does not automatically imply missing coverage. Neighbour progress delays isolated highlights/detail. These are evidence dependencies, not operation quotas, fixed phases or blur-target promotion gates. Placement is a soft scheduling/confidence constraint, not a complete constraint-satisfaction system.

The early Mat/Wonder Lake mechanism was inspected and revised before the full evaluation. Quiet studies initially retained contact islands and systematic gaps. Distinguishing open support ends from reliable boundaries improved coverage; separating broad-model error from unresolved detail then allowed actual narrower transition work instead of endless broad filling. The final studies show progressive shallow-gradient and rounded-form construction, and a dark mass with an open narrow light gap. Residual diagonal seams and grain remain; these are not ideal smoothness demonstrations.

Actual 12/37/101/303/700/1400/final images, including partial tasks, are supplied for every reference. At 37 and101, Mat establishes selective placement and broad negative-space/value relationships across the image. By303, connected tone is much stronger than V64, but cheek/forehead contacts already show slanted facets. At1400/final, narrower modelling changes tone and some boundaries, yet does not resolve those facets or produce convincing facial volume. Ayam's silhouette is recognisable but its dark body is blunt and tail marks are fragmented. Wonder Lake avoids V64's quantisation strips, but its tree/reflection detail becomes too soft and broad directional marks dominate. This is not a successful finish disguised by task counters.

Forest and Rainier were frozen, historically used references **held out from V65 tuning**, not genuinely unseen images. Runtime/dependency hashes were checked before evaluation and remain unchanged. Both held-out drawings are serious failures: coarse planes, weak definition and misplaced/emphasised boundaries remain. They exhaust their bounded proposal allowances before 2800 accepted actions. No retrospective parameter change, budget increase or fallback was used to rescue them.

The held-out rejection cost is a major failure, not an economy benefit inferred from fewer accepted actions.

## Verification and recovery

`node v65-check.cjs`: exact historical oil and charcoal44/50/53/58/63/64 generation/Continue against the pre65 engine; both seeds and all session depths; deterministic bounded tasks, coupled quantities, incremental/full score agreement, local trial supports, sealed targeting, exact Undo and native/2x source-free replay. `node v65-browser.cjs`: actual worker Paint/Pause/Resume/Continue, preserved active identity, targeting/Undo, hidden irrelevant controls, JSON import and pixel-exact native/2x exported PNGs. `node v65-recovery-check.cjs`: complete native paintings against recorded real material, lossless compact documents, frozen historical control streams and genuine partial-task checkpoints. `node v65-review-check.cjs`: all five references, four planners, work/time matching, focused action replay and actual sheet assets offline.

Reproduce in `stroke-master`: prepare supplied originals with `python v64-prepare.py`; run `node v65-study.cjs --studies`, `node v65-study.cjs --early`, then `node v65-study.cjs`; inspect/freeze before `node v65-study.cjs --held-out`. Run development/held-out again with `--baselines` for V64/V50/V58. Timing replacements use `node v65-timings.cjs --mat` and `--v65-other-development`. Build with `node build.cjs`, `node v65-build-review.cjs`, `python v65-sheets.py`, then `python v65-report.py` and `python v65-package.py`.

The review ZIP contains the actual progression, work/time and native crop JPEGs, focused constructions, prepared references, offline runtime and one lossless accepted stream for each complete comparison or study. No duplicated checkpoint streams. Accepted JSON restores artwork/replay/export, not a live optimiser. Bulk diagnostics and full PNG checkpoints remain ignored in `../artifacts/v65-work`; generated review assets remain ignored in `artifacts/v65`.

**Conclusion:** V65 changes coverage, overlap fitting and progression causally. Connected values improve over V64, but selective modelling/finishing and late task rejection remain unconvincing. Preserve the frozen failure evidence; do not promote this planner.
