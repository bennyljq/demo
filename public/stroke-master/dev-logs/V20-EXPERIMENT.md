# V20: retain selective loading as a manual experiment

**Retain V20 experimental; keep V14 default until user approval.** At 2100 accepted operations, the portrait has more connected brow/nose shading and clearer facial planes than V17. The rooster's tail/body colour masses are less fragmented. These gains warrant keeping the bounded comparison option, not a default promotion. Angular cheek patches, distracting fibres and a pale comb-shoulder fringe remain. The controlled smooth transition and blue/ochre construction runs select no loading and match V17 exactly. This experiment does not establish fewer total operations or a general full-painting benefit.

## One extension, capped work

V20 first constructs and scores the same three V17 single-colour candidates. The ordinary contacts have already passed V17's geometry fitting and actual-support validation. Then at most **one** existing Flat/Filbert contact per proposal receives **two** colour fits: balance .5, softness .8 or 2, variation zero. The existing three choices remain available; no candidate is replaced. Only A/B and loading metadata differ, including identical points, width, profile, pressure, opacity, load, taper, depletion, seed, thickness, pigment and target metadata. Foundation is excluded. This is a compute cap, not a gradient quota or a size/acceptance bonus.

The cheap estimate takes nine samples: three arc-length positions (.2/.5/.8), three positions across the actual pressure/taper span (-.65/0/.65). It uses canonical alpha/profile maths and current structural paint to estimate the required deposited colour, rather than trusting a reference gradient alone. Source transition RMS must reach 10 channel units, correction transition RMS 12, sampled alpha must exceed .08, row slopes must be correlated, and centre bends must be small relative to side differences. A weighted lateral regression supplies the selection estimate; it is not a final gradient score. Unsupported profiles, sub-four-pixel contacts, empty contacts, nearly uniform corrections and obvious incoherent/step-like transitions skip fitting. There is no area multiplier or preference for larger contacts. Thresholds and two softness values were fixed before painting assessment and were not widened after seeing zero selections.

The chosen contact has a **16384-pixel bounding-patch cap**. Larger patches skip loading and keep the ordinary candidate. A single canonical alpha/local-coordinate sampling pass collects the complete deposited footprint, including target feathering and weak alpha fringes. The renderer's sampleColour mode shares geometry, projection, footprint/filtering and deposition with painting; it omits RGB/height writes and does not create a parallel renderer. Alpha-only support validation still omits all colour-field work.

For each softness, the existing V19 loadingWeight yields the two regression columns alpha*(1-mix) and alpha*mix. Targets subtract existing paint*(1-alpha). Importance-weighted native structural RGB SSE is fitted; fine pigment texture remains omitted exactly as it is by the production structural surface. A two-variable quadratic has an exact bounded solution: the unconstrained optimum when inside [0,255], otherwise the best point on four box edges. No V19 diagnostic basis/material/structural rerender loop is copied into production. Endpoint RMS below 10 skips a weak fit. Every surviving alternative gets one ordinary evaluateCandidate render and the unchanged full affected footprint/scoring halo. The full production structural objective, not the estimate or a uniform average, decides selection and acceptance.

Support results are reused because deposited colour cannot change alpha/coverage/height and all non-colour fields are identical. The invalid V19 boundary fixture still fails with and without loading. No better colour fit can make that original geometry admissible. There is no new clipping, underlay, orientation/longitudinal search, palette restriction, wet mixing or general optimiser.

## Selection and visual findings

Portrait: 6546 screened, 116 eligible, 76 fit attempts, four maximum-patch skips; 152 loaded alternatives evaluated, 143 beating their own ordinary counterpart and 46 accepted. All 152 have positive objective gain; 106 lose to another offered choice. The mechanism is limited mainly by eligibility and competition, not widespread nonpositive loaded colour scores. Thirteen accepted loaded centres fall in the predefined face rectangle and two in its cheek crop. These spatial counts are diagnostics only, never planner coordinates/rules. Accepted loads are 38 construction-pass and eight shapes-pass contacts, none in detail passes.

Rooster: 6546 screened, 82 eligible, 56 fit attempts, 112 endpoint fits, four weak fits skipped, 108 canonical loaded evaluations, 100 better than their own ordinary contact, 32 accepted and 76 positive alternatives losing selection. Loads are 31 construction-pass and one shapes-pass contact. Controlled transition and blue/ochre each have zero eligible contacts, zero fits and exact V17 strokes/RNG/buffers. At these native sizes, the unchanged fine planner offers much narrower contacts than V19's wide fixed-contact proof; the cross-width transition/correction gate is not met. Zero selection is an eligibility result, not a weak-fit or objective rejection. No orientation search, threshold adjustment or budget expansion was added to make those cases win.

At construction, V20 already models the portrait nose plane and some background/tree tones with coherent gradients, while both sides remain deliberately sparse. At 1800/final, the brow and nose shading connect more clearly, the eye/lip cues remain useful, and the hair/body outline stays readable. Bright angular cheek patches remain distracting; the treatment does not globally smooth them. At approximately matched work, the brow/nose benefit remains visible but cheek patchiness does not disappear. Native pigment shows less contradictory tone around the left brow than control; Neutral still exposes pre-existing relief ridges. Native/2x replay reveals the same loading but larger outputs retain conspicuous fibre detail, so this does not solve preview/export texture differences.

The rooster's tail and dark body masses are more connected; the foot/toe gaps remain open in the review crops. The comb's left shoulder has a visible pale fringe in treatment, and the head accents move with downstream ordinary choices. Thus unchanged validation is not proof of improved native outline discipline. The retained coarse-compatibility/high-resolution limits remain material concerns; no boundary fix is claimed. Blue/ochre broad construction and the controlled transition are unchanged, including their existing coverage/facet limitations.

Accepted small-operation counts do **not** fall: portrait 1669 -> 1671, rooster 1784 -> 1789 (foundation excluded by existing scale metrics). The visual benefit is fewer contradictory colour breaks in selected broad regions, not fewer small contacts or a proven reduction in total brush work. Whole objective decreases 18.50% on portrait and 11.64% on rooster, but this numerical result is secondary to these specific visual findings and limitations.
