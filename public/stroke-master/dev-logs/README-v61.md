# V61 - coherent long charcoal gestures

V61 expands supported gesture range from exact frozen V58, including its blending. It remains experimental: complete comparisons do **not** demonstrate a quality, operation-economy or compute advantage. V44 remains default; V50 Baseline and V58 Benchmark retain their historical behaviour. No promotion or commit.

There was no universal 0.1-canvas renderer limit. V58's guided proposals take at most 12 steps per side at approximately long-edge/160: a 0.15 centreline ceiling. Residual, value, canvas and structural-profile checks shorten that further. Ordinary V53-derived opportunities already nominally permit 0.30 broad / 0.34 narrow travel; their width-coupled fallback can be shorter. The native renderer accepts eight path nodes, varying width/pressure/angle and sampled Catmull-Rom curves. It deposits a finite face along the path rather than stretching a rectangle. Entry/release, loading and broken paper contact can shorten visible extent independently of the centreline.
